Executive Profile

About Al

Al Broderick is a battle-tested Chief Revenue Officer and fractional sales executive with a career built on one foundational truth: revenue is never accidental. From the discipline of eight years serving in the United States Army to leading multimillion-dollar enterprise deals at Lenovo — one of the world's most recognized technology brands — Al has spent three decades transforming sales organizations, building high-performance teams, and engineering the kind of revenue growth that compounds year over year.

Armed with an MBA, a Certified Federal Contracts Manager (CFCM) designation, and deep sector expertise spanning enterprise technology, cybersecurity, AI governance, and federal contracting, Al brings a rare combination of operational rigor and executive vision. As a Fractional CRO with Go Fractional, he now partners with growth-stage companies and established enterprises alike — delivering the strategic horsepower of a seasoned Chief Revenue Officer without the full-time overhead. His clients gain a revenue architect who has been in the room, closed the deal, and built the team.

A proud US Army veteran, bilingual in English and Spanish, and longtime volunteer with the Food Bank of North Carolina, the Veteran Mentor Network, and the NC Pro Bono Resource Center, Al leads with the same commitment off the field as on it. Based in Raleigh, North Carolina, he is available for fractional CRO engagements, board advisory roles, and executive keynotes.

Career Timeline

A Career Built on Execution

Three decades across military service, federal contracting, enterprise technology, and executive leadership.

1987–1995

Military Service — United States Army

Eight years of active duty forged the leadership principles Al carries into every boardroom: mission clarity, team accountability, and executing under pressure. The Army didn't just build a soldier — it built a strategist.

1995–2020

Federal Contracting & Clinical Research

Earning his CFCM designation and Clinical Research Professional credential, Al mastered the art of complex, high-stakes deal structuring in government and regulated industries — managing multimillion-dollar proposals, navigating cross-functional stakeholders, and winning in markets where precision is non-negotiable.

2010–2024

Enterprise Sales — Fortune 500 Technology

Al ascended through enterprise tech sales, ultimately earning recognition as SSG Seller of the Year at Lenovo — one of the world's largest technology companies. He co-hosted Lenovo's North America Sales Kickoff, partnered with Microsoft and CDW on recognized Corporate, SMB, M/M, and Public Sector initiatives, and drove category-defining revenue in competitive markets.

2015–2021

Legal Sales & Contracts Operations — Cisco Systems

At Cisco — a 40+ year global enterprise powerhouse that connects people and organizations through technology leveraging its hardware, software, and service offerings to create reliable Internet solutions that make networks possible — Al brought his contracting expertise to bear within a high-complexity legal sales and operations environment, managing intricate agreements and strengthening cross-functional partnerships at the enterprise level.

2018–Present

Chief Revenue Officer — Go Fractional / Liontec Solutions Group

Today, Al deploys decades of earned expertise as a Fractional CRO at Go Fractional & Liontec Solutions Group, building revenue architectures for companies navigating the intersection of AI, cybersecurity, and enterprise growth. His clients get a seasoned executive who has closed the deals, led the teams, and built the playbooks — exactly when they need it.
